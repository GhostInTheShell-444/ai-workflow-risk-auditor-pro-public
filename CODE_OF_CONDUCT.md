# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, ability, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and technically honest.
- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Accept feedback and correct mistakes.
- Protect privacy; never share another person's private information.
- Use synthetic data in project discussions and demonstrations.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, trolling, sustained disruption, doxxing, or publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through repository maintainer or moderation channels. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and fairly as practical.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

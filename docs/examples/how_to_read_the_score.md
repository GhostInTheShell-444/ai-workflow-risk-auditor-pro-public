# How To Read The Score

## The short version

Higher score means more local risk clues were mapped from the workflow text. It does not mean a real-world incident is certain.

## Scale

- 0-4 = Low
- 5-9 = Medium
- 10-15 = High
- 16+ = Critical

## Detected

Detected means the app found evidence in the text, such as "customer email" or "human review".

## Calculated

Calculated means the app used local deterministic rules to add risk-factor weights.

## Simulated

Simulated means the app estimated what could happen after selected protections. This is not a guarantee.

## Recommended

Recommended means the app proposes an action. A responsible human decides what is required.

## Uncertain

Uncertain means a human must review the evidence and context.

This score is a rule-based estimate, not a statistical probability. Score calculated from local deterministic rules. It is an aid, not a certification.
